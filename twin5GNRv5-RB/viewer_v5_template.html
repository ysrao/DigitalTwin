Current and change

Throughput is the mean per user over every cell of that class inside the radius, with unserved cells counted as zero. With load at 0% it is single-user throughput on an empty cell. With a load ρ, each user gets (1 − ρ) of it (processor sharing). DL service needs SINR ≥ −8.7 dB (control channel). UL service needs the UE to close a 1-RB PUSCH at −0.7 dB.

Device classes and model terms

Common to all classes: UE noise figure 7 dB; gNB noise figure 3 dB and target rise over thermal 4 dB (workbook). Handset antenna follows the workbook fit 6.7292·ln(fMHz) − 51.61, capped at 2.14 dBi. Efficiency uses the workbook's 36.942 CQI curves, the 2×2 SU-MIMO curve and the Vendor-B 4×4 curve, with the workbook's DL overhead (normal CP, 3 PDCCH symbols, 2 or 4 ports) and UL overhead 22.34%. TDD special slots are 10:2:2 symbols. Foliage uses Weissberger's model on the line-of-sight share of power (capped at 20 dB) with NLCD 2021 canopy cover and 8 m trees. Ray tracing: max depth 3, specular + diffuse (scattering 0.3 buildings, 0.2 ground) + edge diffraction, 1M rays per source.

Data: USGS 3DEP elevation (public domain), SanGIS building outlines (license check pending per DATA_LICENSES.md), building heights from the planner's LiDAR-derived raster, NLCD 2021 Tree Canopy Cover (USFS/MRLC, public domain). Single tower: no neighbour cells. Use the DL interference input to approximate them.
