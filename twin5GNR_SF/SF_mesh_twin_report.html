twin5GNR on the Vexcel San Francisco mesh — first run

Area: downtown San Francisco (Chinatown / Nob Hill), tileset centre 37.801105, −122.404175; local ENU extent x [-484, 484] m, y [-482, 482] m. Scene: 1024 L20 tiles, 1,946,731 vertices, 1,196,963 faces, converted from the Vexcel 1 km² Cesium eval set.

Configuration (same conventions as twin5GNR v5)

Transmitter at the tile centre, 30 m above the local ground surface (stated assumption — no SF site data was supplied). 3 sectors (azimuth 90/210/330°), TR 38.901 element pattern, 4×4 array, tilts 0–10° (results shown at 4°), 47 dBm boresight EIRP. Solver: Sionna RT 2.2.0 PathSolver, max_depth 3, LOS + specular + diffuse (Lambertian) + diffraction + edge diffraction, 1M samples/source. Material: the fused Vexcel mesh carries a single ITU concrete radio material with scattering coefficient S = 0.3 (assumption — the mesh does not separate ground, buildings and vegetation). Receiver grids: 25×25 at 40 m spacing. Roof = surface + 6 m (rooftop CPE). Street = ground + 1.5 m (outdoor handset), points inside building footprints nudged to the nearest open cell within 30 m; ground from a morphological opening of the mesh surface.

Vegetation — ITU-R P.833-10 (modelled, post-trace)

Vegetation is computed post-trace, not inside Sionna RT: the fused Vexcel mesh carries no separate vegetation layer, so foliage loss is applied afterwards to the LOS component of each cell, weighted by its LOS power share (φ), using the foliage depth d (m) along the straight TX–RX line. No Sionna re-run.

Canopy source (surveyed positions): City of San Francisco Street Tree List (DataSF dataset tkzw-k3nq), 1,919 public street trees in the 1 km tile. Top species: Indian Laurel Fig 261, London Plane 244, Victorian Box 148, Brisbane Box 125, Banyan Fig 69. Median trunk diameter (DBH) 11 in. Each tree is modelled as a canopy disc; the TX–RX line is sampled every 5 m and path length inside a disc is accumulated while the line height is within that tree’s crown range. Foliage depth, street: mean 3.09 m, median 0.00 m, p90 10.05 m, max 30.24 m, 37.3% of cells > 0; roof: mean 1.18 m.

Model: ITU-R P.833-10 Eq. (1), Aev = Am[1 − exp(−dγ/Am)], where γ is specific attenuation (dB/m) and Am the maximum attenuation (dB). Parameters are interpolated/assumed, uncalibrated (from P.833-10 Table 1 / Fig. 2 and 28 GHz forest measurements):

Roof (CPE): 3.6 GHz delta ~0 in every bin; 28 GHz mean DL delta −0.0 / −20.6 / −29.9 / −28.2 Mbps by bin (0–100 / 100–250 / 250–500 / 500–679 m). Median RSRP delta is 0.0 dB in every bin at both bands — the median cell has no foliage on its direct path; the effect lives in the tail.

Verdict: vegetation matters only at 28 GHz, street level, 100–500 m; ~0 at 3.6 GHz at any distance, ~0 at 0–100 m and past 500 m, ~0 for rooftops.

Limitations: public street trees only — no private-yard or park trees, so this is a lower bound on total canopy. Crown size/height are assumed from DBH (radius 2–5 m, crown 2–12 m); the inventory has no crown measurements. No terrain DEM — height above ground is a linear interpolation from 30 m at the TX to 1.5 m (street) / 6 m (roof) at the RX. The 40 m grid is coarse — tree crowns (3–6 m) are sub-cell, so this is a sensitivity test; at 10 cm resolution, crowns are resolved and per-tree RET becomes meaningful. Sameer Lalwani is the intended user of this vegetation model at his resolution. At our map resolution — a 40 m receiver grid on a Vexcel mesh of ~1 m facets, with canopy data at ~25 m (NLCD) or surveyed tree points — a 3–6 m tree crown is sub-cell, so foliage is applied statistically per cell, not per tree.

Provenance

Simulated — path gains, LOS and diffuse shares: Sionna RT 2.2.0 ray tracing on the Vexcel mesh scene, settings above.
Modelled — RSRP and DL throughput: derived from the simulated path gains with the stated EIRP, bandwidth, antenna and workbook terms (twin_model.py, the same code as the twin5GNR v5 viewer).
Not computed — multi-cell interference / SINR under load; indoor receivers (street grid is outdoor at the facade); the wider 5×5 km area (only the 1 km² eval set is in hand).
Vegetation — now computed post-trace with ITU-R P.833-10 from the surveyed street-tree inventory (see Vegetation section); the fused mesh itself still carries no separate vegetation layer in Sionna RT.
Assumptions to calibrate — concrete-only material, S = 0.3 scattering, TX height 30 m AGL at the tile centre, ground/street placement from the mesh-derived opening.
