01Two timescales

Everything runs on a fast tick and a slow control interval. Each tick (Δt = 0.1 s by default) advances UE positions along their velocity vectors, recomputes RSRP, and steps the A3/A5 measurement state machine. Once per control interval (100 ticks = 10 s) the policy chooses an action, PRBs are allocated, and the interval's KPIs are aggregated. One step() = one control interval; T intervals make an episode.

02Offered demand

Each session carries a base rate from its type (eMBB 5, URLLC 1, mIoT 0.02 Mbps). A per-slice load factor λs(t) scales those rates over the episode. Its shape is one of: flat (λ ≡ 1); diurnal, a two-peak day; or a named profile taken from the platform's established trace synthetic_profiles/all_profiles.csv (P1…P5 — busy hour, eMBB hotspot, URLLC burst, massive IoT, mixed-failure stress), normalised to its own peak. A carrier trace drops into that file without touching anything else.

(1) diurnal:  shape(t) = 0.55 + 0.32·sin(φ − 1.9) + 0.18·sin(2φ − 0.4)
profile:  shapes(t) = traces[t mod T] / maxt traces
  φ = 2π·(t mod T) / T
(2) ρt = 0.85·ρt−1 + 𝒩(0, σ2),  σ = traffic_noise
λs(t) = shapes(t) · (1 + ρt)   →   Di = rate(typei) · λs(i)(t)

mIoT is held less diurnal (a 0.45 + 0.55·shape floor).

03Propagation & channel

Per tier, path loss comes from the tier's morphology (UMa / UMi / RMa / InH → TR 38.901; NTN → TR 38.811). The terrestrial LOS branch, below the breakpoint distance:

(3) PLLOS = 28.0 + 22·log10(d3D) + 20·log10(fc)  [UMa]

LOS and NLOS are blended by the TR 38.901 LOS probability in the linear domain, so a single distance-indexed table per tier is exact:

(4) PL = −10·log10( pLOS·10−PLLOS/10 + (1−pLOS)·10−PLNLOS/10 )

Indoor UEs served by an outdoor cell add the TR 38.901 O2I term (low or high loss). Each link also carries spatially-correlated log-normal shadow fading — one value per (session, tier), σ from the TR 38.901 tables (≈ 6–8 dB), evolving as a Gudmundson AR(1) process with the distance the UE moves:

(5) ξt = e−Δd/dcorr·ξt−1 + 1 − e−2Δd/dcorr·σ·𝒩(0,1)   dcorr ≈ 50 m
RSRP = EIRPRE − PL − LO2I − Lblockage − ξt

SINR is against thermal noise and co-channel interference — the sum of received power from same-band cells other than the server, each weighted by its load ρj (activity) and the tier's beam-coupling κ (1.0 omni, 0.35 upper-mid-band, 0.08 mmWave). 12 REs per PRB:

(5′) N0 = −174 + 10·log10(BPRB·106) + NF
I = 12 · Σj ≠ serv, same band Prx,jRE · ρj · κj
SINR = Prx,servPRB10N0/10 + I   (κ = 0 ⇒ noise-limited, the old model)

Non-terrestrial (LEO)

Slant range at elevation ε over a spherical Earth (R = 6371 km, h = altitude), then free-space loss:

(6) d = (R·sin ε)2 + 2R·h + h2 − R·sin ε
FSPL = 92.45 + 20·log10(fGHz) + 20·log10(dkm)

Rain attenuation follows ITU-R P.618 with the P.838 coefficients and P.839 rain height; it is negligible at S-band and dominant at Ka-band:

(7) γR = k·Rrainα  [dB/km]    Arain = γR · LS · r0.01

The LEO pass sweeps elevation εmin → 90° → εmin; Doppler and its residual after pre-compensation drive inter-carrier interference:

(8) fd = fc·ṙ / c     εoff = Δfres / SCS     ICI/S ≈ (π·εoff)23

Per-stream spectral efficiency is Shannon, capped, and de-rated for mobility; MIMO multiplies delivered cell capacity:

(9) se = min( log2(1 + SINR), semax ) · ηmob · (1 − ICI/S)
Ccell,s = nPRB,s · BPRB · se · GMIMO
(10) GMIMO = max(1, L) · ηMIMO · (1.15 if MU else 1.0)
nPRB = ⌊ 0.95 · BW · NCC / (12·SCS) ⌋  if not given explicitly

04Association & handover

Each UE holds a measurement set of its nearest cells (plus the satellite beam, always). A steering eligibility mask is applied first: indoor-only cells serve indoor UEs, outdoor-only (the LEO beam) serve outdoor UEs, a tier cannot hold a UE faster than its vmax, and the satellite is reserved for v > vNTN or a best-terrestrial RSRP below the coverage-hole threshold.

A3/A5 events are evaluated on biased levels M = RSRP + CIO + steering bias. Entry conditions (TS 38.331), held for the time-to-trigger:

(11) A3:  Mn − Hys > Ms + Off
A5:  Ms + Hys < T1  ∧  Mn − Hys > T2

Off, Hys, TTT, T1, T2 are per session type: URLLC uses a short 40 ms trigger, mIoT a long 1280 ms trigger with a large offset.

A fired handover can still fail. Probability of a late (failed) handover rises with UE speed and falls with the trigger margin:

(12) Plate = clip( 0.01 + 0.10·(v/120) − 0.01·Δmargin,  0,  0.6 )

Also tracked: admission refusal (target at capacity), speed rejection, ping-pong (return to the previous cell inside a 5 s window), radio-link failure (Ms < Mmin), and the interruption time — which breaks continuity when it exceeds the class's tolerance.

05Scheduling & KPIs

Each tier declares which of the three slices it carries. A full terrestrial tier serves {eMBB, URLLC, mIoT}; mmwave drops mIoT; wifi7_indoor and ntn_leo drop URLLC — a ~25 ms LEO service-link RTT cannot underwrite a 5 ms budget. The policy's per-tier template πtier is masked to that set and renormalised (πs ← πs·ms / Σ π·m), then PRBs are shared equally among the sessions of each slice on the cell. Served rate per session is min(demand, capacity), capped by backhaul. Aggregated per slice:

(13) σs = clip( Σ serveds / max(Σ offereds, ε),  0,  1 )
SLAs = 𝟙[ offereds ≤ ε  ∨  σs ≥ θs ]   θ = (0.95, 0.999, 0.99)

Unmet demand is split by cause — the distinction the feasibility check needs:

(14) gapcov,s = Σ demand of sessions with no eligible tier
gapcap,s = Σ (demand − served) over attached sessions

Jain fairness over the three slice satisfactions:

(15) F = ( Σ σs )2N · Σ σs2

06Reward

One scalar per control interval. Satisfaction is weighted toward URLLC; utilisation and fairness are small positive terms; SLA violation, energy, handover churn and lost continuity are penalties.

χ = ( nping-pong + nRLF ) / Nsessions
(17) r = w·σ + 0.06·U + 0.04·F − 0.12·V − cE·Efrac − cHO·χ − cC·(1 − C)

07Learning

Observation: a 46-D vector — demand, phase, utilisation, continuity, handover stats, NTN elevation and rain, plus six features per tier. Action is per tier: one of nine slice-mix templates for each tier, plus one global steering posture — a multi-categorical action with ntiers + 1 softmax heads on a shared 46 → 64 → 64 trunk. Single agent, one update.

PPO

The joint log-probability is the sum over branches; the clipped surrogate acts on the joint ratio, with GAE(λ) advantages.

(18) ρt = exp( Σk log πk(ak,t) − log πold,t )
Lclip = −𝔼[ min( ρt·Ât,  clip(ρt, 1−ϵ, 1+ϵ)·Ât ) ]
(19) δt = rt + γ·V(st+1)·(1−dt) − V(st)
Ât = δt + γ·λ·(1−dt)·Ât+1   γ = 0.95, λ = 0.95, ϵ = 0.2

Double DQN (branching)

Per-tier Q heads; the online net picks each branch's action, the target net scores it, and the per-branch bootstraps are averaged into one shared TD target (BDQN). Huber loss.

(20) yt = rt + γ·(1−dt)·1K Σk Qtgt,k( st+1,  argmaxa Qon,k(st+1, a) )

08Optimisation & feasibility

Coordinate descent over a knob set chosen by objective — handover · throughput · latency · coverage. Each objective is scored on its own KPI, always net of a session-continuity guard, so nothing can win by stranding sessions:

(21) guard = 2·(1 − C) + 1·rateping-pong
scorelat = −latency / 5  +  0.5·σURLLC  −  guard

Knobs: per-class A3/A5 thresholds, per-tier cell individual offset CIO, steering thresholds, per-class Mmin.

Feasibility verdict Every run reports whether threshold tuning can even help. With gcov = Σ gapcov / Σ offered and gcap = Σ gapcap / Σ offered:
gcov > 0.05  →  COVERAGE-LIMITED  (needs sites / low-band / NTN)
gcap > 0.10 ∧ headroom < 0.1·Σoffered  →  CAPACITY-LIMITED  (needs spectrum / cells)
otherwise  →  FEASIBLE  (tune thresholds)
thresholds_can_help is true only for FEASIBLE.

09Peak vs loaded capacity

Two capacity figures are reported. Peak assumes full PRB use, every UE at the tier's SE ceiling, no interference — a hardware upper bound. Loaded substitutes an interference-limited SE from a hex reuse-1 screening estimate: six first-ring interferers at √3·r from the deployed cell density, each at 50% activity, scaled by κ, averaged over a cell-edge and a mid-cell point.

(22) r = A / (Ncells·π)  nominal cell radius
SINRloaded = Prx(0.7r)N0 + 6·Prx(√3·r)·κ·0.5   Cloaded = nPRB·BPRB·SEloaded·GMIMO

Coverage is the fraction of area within the link-budget radius of a cell, at the deployed density, with a 1.28σ shadow-fade allowance and a 5% inter-cell edge outage — so a well-covered tier reads ≈ 95%, not 100%.

10Not yet in the model

Open PRACH / random-access overload — no preamble-capacity limit or contention, so an mIoT arrival storm never causes access failure and mIoT / URLLC connections-per-cell are shown but not enforced. Per-KPI targets as inputs — eMBB throughput, URLLC latency and mIoT coverage-per-km² are internal constants, not scenario knobs. RIC interfaces — §11's O-RAN placement is conceptual only: no A1AP or E2AP protocol stack, no real E2 Node, no SMO or near-RT RIC platform sits in the loop.

Closed since v1: co-channel interference in the SINR (§3), spatially-correlated shadow fading (§3), and trace-driven time-varying offered load (§2).

New in v4: §11 RIC/xApp placement — the non-RT / near-RT RIC correspondence, an A1/E2 mapping for each existing component, and an explicit line between what is architectural framing and what is actually implemented.

11RIC placement: where this controller would sit

The controller in §§04–07 is a standalone policy, not an O-RAN component — but it is worth being precise about where it would sit if it were one. The O-RAN Alliance splits RAN intelligence into two controllers with different cadences. The non-RT RIC lives in the SMO, runs on a >1 s loop, hosts rApps, and sends policy guidance down over the A1 interface. The near-RT RIC runs a 10 ms – 1 s loop, hosts xApps, and talks to the RAN's E2 Nodes (gNB-CU/DU) over E2, using service models such as E2SM-KPM for KPI monitoring and E2SM-RC for control actions.

As implemented, not as it could be The twin's control interval is 10 s (100 ticks × 100 ms, §1) — three to four orders of magnitude coarser than the 10 ms – 1 s near-RT loop. As built, this maps onto a non-RT RIC / rApp-style policy-tuning loop — adjusting per-tier, per-slice weights on a ~10 s cadence — not onto a near-RT xApp making per-TTI, per-UE scheduling decisions. Calling the current controller "an xApp" without that caveat would overclaim what it does.

What a real near-RT placement would add

Pushing the controller down to a true near-RT xApp — future work, not implemented here — would mean control actions resolved per-UE / per-cell instead of per-tier, expressed as E2SM-RC action messages instead of a slice-mix template; reward attribution fast enough to fit inside a 10 ms – 1 s decision window instead of a 10 s one; and a real E2AP protocol stack talking to real E2 Nodes instead of the twin's own simulated tiers. None of that exists in this codebase today.
