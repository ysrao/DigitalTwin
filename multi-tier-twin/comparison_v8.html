Not statistically significant — and the mechanism isn't what it looks like

Cooperative MARL shows a small aggregate reward advantage (0.5021 → 0.5226, +4.08%), but the training-seed-clustered 95% interval for that difference is [−0.0069, +0.0625] — it crosses zero, and MARL wins on only 4 of 10 training seeds. Per-seed analysis shows the gap is explained by a training-reliability effect (MARL reliably finds the same corner allocation; centralized PPO occasionally stalls in a worse local optimum), not by richer adaptive coordination.

MARL's reward is identical to four decimal places across all 10 training seeds — it converges to the same corner solution every time. The centralized controller varies (0.3451–0.5338) and only loses to MARL on the 4 seeds where it stalls in a worse local optimum.

Why: a corner solution, not adaptive control

On the tested profile (P1_balanced_busy_hour), URLLC and mIoT/eMTC offered demand is small relative to capacity even at their protected floor shares, so both sit at 100% satisfaction under almost any policy. The only allocation decision that matters is the eMBB share, and the reward-maximizing policy collapses toward one near-static corner allocation. MARL's three small, decomposed 7-input/5-action subproblems find that corner reliably in 10/10 training seeds. The centralized controller, searching a larger 13-input/9-way joint space, finds an equal-or-better solution in 6/10 seeds but stalls in a markedly worse local optimum in the other 4 — which is what produces the aggregate gap. This is a training-reliability difference, not evidence of richer per-slice coordination.

What was compared

  • Centralized PPO: one 13-input, 64×64 PPO actor–critic (the project's existing ActorCritic, unmodified) chooses one of 9 discrete joint PRB-share templates.
  • Cooperative MARL: three independent 7-input, 64×64 ActorCritic instances — one per slice — each choosing one of 5 discrete bid levels; a deterministic coordinator projects the three bids onto the shared PRB budget and slice floors (0.25, 0.20, 0.10). All three receive the same cooperative reward.
  • Matched controls: 260 training episodes × 48 MDP steps (12,480 interactions) per training seed, seeds 1301–1310, PPO clip 0.2, γ=0.97, GAE λ=0.95; evaluated on 40 held-out traffic windows per trained pair (seeds 5001–5040), 400 paired evaluations.
  • Statistics: training-seed-clustered bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, for the 95% CI of the reward difference.
