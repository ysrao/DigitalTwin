A tier's cells are shared across the slices it carries by the policy's per-tier PRB template, so the eMBB throughput column is the tier's full capacity available to eMBB, not a reservation. URLLC and mIoT are shown in their binding unit — connections / devices per cell — not Gbit. Coverage is a radio-geometry property of the tier, identical for every slice it serves.

How these numbers are calculated

Per-cell peak throughput (an upper bound — full PRB use, every UE at the tier's spectral-efficiency ceiling, no interference, no scheduling loss):

peak_cell = PRBs × PRB_BW_MHz × SE_max × G_MIMO

  • PRBs = ⌊ 0.95 × BW_MHz × carriers ÷ PRB_BW_MHz ⌋, or an explicit value
  • PRB_BW_MHz = 12 × SCS_kHz ÷ 1000  (SCS 30 kHz → 0.36 MHz)
  • SE_max = the tier's peak spectral efficiency, bit/s/Hz (3.0 low-band … 8.0 mmWave)
  • G_MIMO = max(1, layers) × 0.75 × (1.15 if MU else 1.0)  (4×MU → 3.45)

tier capacity = peak_cell × cells  ·  network capacity = Σ tier capacity

Area covered = min(1, cells × π·r² ÷ area). The cell radius r is the largest distance whose TR 38.901 path loss still fits the link budget:

MAPL = Tx + G_tx + G_rx − (−174 + 10·log₁₀(PRB_BW·10⁶) + NF) − SINR_req − margin

Found by bisection on the LOS-probability-blended path loss. No interference term, so radii and capacity are optimistic upper bounds. NTN range is the TR 38.811 slant range at the minimum elevation. Achieved throughput from Run comparison is far lower.

Why URLLC and mIoT are shown per cell, not in Gbit: URLLC is a latency/reliability slice — you typically carry only a few URLLC connections per cell, each with a small bit-rate and a hard latency budget, so the binding number is connections/cell and the access latency of the serving tier, not aggregate Gbit. The mIoT figure is only offered device load. PRACH contention, preamble collisions, retries, access barring and admission limits are not modelled in v2; this console therefore does not estimate mIoT random-access capacity or storm performance.
